Section 2 · Useful Expressions

Functional Language — Useful ExpressionsCác mẫu câu nêu ý kiến, đồng ý và không đồng ý

Here are the essential expressions used in the video to state opinions, agree, and disagree with others.

💬

1. Stating Opinion (Nêu ý kiến)

Pattern Example
I think (that)...
I think housework is important for children.
In my opinion,...
In my opinion, kids should help at home.
I believe (that)...
I believe simple chores are easy to manage.
Cả 3 cấu trúc trên đều có thể thay thế nhau tự nhiên khi bạn muốn mở đầu một ý kiến cá nhân.
👍

2. Agreeing (Đồng ý)

Pattern Example
I agree (with you).
I agree with you, chores are useful.
That's right.
That's right, everyone should help a little.
"That's right" hợp khi xác nhận lại điều vừa nghe; "I agree with you" dùng khi chia sẻ cùng quan điểm.
✋

3. Disagreeing (Không đồng ý)

Pattern Example
I don't think so.
I don't think so, kids need more free time.
I don't agree.
I don't agree, homework should come first.
⚠️ Hai cấu trúc trên dùng để phản bác nhẹ nhàng và lịch sự trong giao tiếp hàng ngày.

Section 4 · Sentence Structure

Expressing Reasons — Should vs. Shouldn'tCấu trúc đưa ra lý do — Nên / Không nên làm việc nhà

Giving an opinion is not enough — support your point of view with specific reasons.

Children should do housework because... Children shouldn't do housework because...
...it makes them feel proud of contributing something real to the family.
...their schedules are already packed with classes and extracurricular activities.
...it gives them practical experience that textbooks can't teach.
...some tasks, like using cleaning chemicals, aren't safe for young children.
...it helps them understand how much effort it takes to run a home.
...it can take time away from hobbies that help them grow in other ways.
