Strategy Notice
Part 2.2
Now let's learn the trick to finding the topic quickly.
Bây giờ hãy cùng học bí quyết để tìm chủ đề thật nhanh.
💡 WHAT
What is the topic?
The topic is what a paragraph is about — usually just a word or a short phrase.
CHỦ ĐỀ là gì? — Chủ đề là điều mà đoạn văn nói đến — thường chỉ là một từ hoặc một cụm từ ngắn.
🎯 WHY
WHY do we need to find it?
We identify the topic to quickly understand what a paragraph is generally talking about — before going into details.
TẠI SAO cần tìm? — Để nhanh chóng hiểu đoạn văn đang nói chung về điều gì, trước khi đi vào chi tiết.
⚡ HOW (4 STEPS)
How do we find it? (4 Steps)
① Read the paragraph once.
② Notice words that repeat or belong to the same group.
③ Ask: "What do these words have in common?"
④ Sum it up in one word or a short phrase.
① Đọc đoạn văn một lượt.
② Chú ý các từ lặp lại hoặc cùng nhóm nghĩa.
③ Tự hỏi: "Những từ này có điểm gì chung?"
④ Tóm gọn lại thành một từ hoặc một cụm từ ngắn.

Part 5.1
Volunteering has become increasingly popular among high school students across the country. Many teens now spend their weekends helping at local shelters, planting trees in public parks, or teaching younger children basic reading and writing skills. Schools often encourage this kind of activity because it teaches responsibility, teamwork, and empathy for others in the community. Teachers say that students who volunteer regularly tend to manage their time better and communicate more confidently with adults outside their family.
Beyond building good habits, volunteering also looks impressive on university applications, especially for students applying to competitive programs abroad. Some students even discover future career interests through their volunteer work. For example, a student who enjoys helping younger children with homework might later decide to become a teacher, while another who spends time at an animal shelter might consider a career in veterinary science. Universities increasingly value applicants who can show real commitment to their community, not just good grades.
Community centers have reported a steady rise in teenage volunteers over the past three years, showing that this trend continues to grow rather than fade away. Many organizations now offer flexible volunteering schedules, allowing students to fit their community service around exams and extracurricular activities. Parents, too, have noticed positive changes in their children's attitudes toward helping others after they begin volunteering regularly, and some now volunteer alongside their children on weekends.
